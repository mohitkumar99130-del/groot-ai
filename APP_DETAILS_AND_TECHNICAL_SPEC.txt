1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
====================================================================================================
Traditional agriculture suffers from fragmented diagnostic signals:
- Satellite imagery alone has high latency (5-day revisits) and cannot detect subsurface root moisture deficits or microscopic fungal spores.
- Ground IoT sensors alone provide single-point data without field-wide spatial coverage.
- Manual farmer field inspections are subjective, slow, and often diagnose blast/rust diseases only after irreversible foliar damage (>40% yield loss).

GROOT (Hara Bhara Planet) solves this trilemma by fusing Satellite Earth Observation (Sentinel-2), Subsoil Mesh Telemetry (ESP32), and On-Field Smartphone Camera Vision (Edge MobileNet-v3) into a single, unified, real-time diagnostic and prescription engine with high-clarity voice assistance in vernacular Indian languages.

3. TRI-MODAL AI FUSION ENGINE (MATHEMATICAL FORMULATIONS & LOGIC)
====================================================================================================
Located at: `src/services/fusionEngine.ts`

The fusion engine runs locally in the browser with sub-millisecond execution time, re-computing composite health, risk percentage, and prescriptions whenever any input modality changes.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Modality 1: Geospatial Sentinel-2 Radiometry (Input: FieldZone)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sentinel-2 Level-2A Multi-Spectral Instrument (MSI) optical data is mapped across a 5x5 grid (25 hectares, Zone A1 to Zone E5).

Mathematical Radiometric Indices:
1. NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index):
   Formula: NDVI = (NIR - RED) / (NIR + RED)
   Bands  : Sentinel-2 Band 8 (842 nm) & Band 4 (665 nm)
   Scale  : 0.0 (bare soil / water) to 1.0 (dense healthy canopy)
   Logic  : NDVI < 0.45 indicates severe vegetative degradation or necrosis.

2. NDMI (Normalized Difference Moisture Index):
   Formula: NDMI = (NIR - SWIR) / (NIR + SWIR)
   Bands  : Sentinel-2 Band 8 (842 nm) & Band 11 (1610 nm)
   Scale  : -0.2 (extreme water stress) to +0.6 (high canopy water content)
   Logic  : NDMI < 0.10 flags acute leaf dehydration.

3. Thermal Surface Radiation:
   Estimated canopy surface temperature in degrees Celsius (°C).
   Logic  : Surface Temp > 32°C indicates stomatal closure and transpiration blockage.

Satellite Risk Computation:
S_risk = max(0, min(100, (1 - NDVI) * 60 + max(0, (0.3 - NDMI) * 80) + max(0, (surfaceTemp - 28) * 3)))

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 Modality 2: In-Situ Subsoil IoT Sensor Mesh (Input: SensorTelemetry)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Simulated ESP32 LoRa mesh node packets placed at 15cm root depth.

Variables:
- VWC (Volumetric Water Content) : 0% - 100% (Capacitive sensor)
- Soil Temperature               : °C (DS18B20 digital probe)
- Soil pH                        : 0 - 14 (Glass electrode sensor)
- Soil EC (Electrical Conduct.)  : mS/cm (Salinity indicator)
- N-P-K Subsoil Concentration    : mg/kg (Optical spectral probe)

IoT Risk Computation:
Moisture Deficit Risk = if (VWC < 25%) -> (25 - VWC) * 2.8
                        if (VWC > 80%) -> (VWC - 80) * 2.0 (Waterlogging)
                        else -> 0
Thermal Risk          = max(0, (SoilTemp - 30) * 3.5)
pH Penalty            = abs(Soil_pH - 6.5) * 12
I_risk = max(0, min(100, Moisture_Risk + Thermal_Risk + pH_Penalty))

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 Modality 3: Edge AI Leaf Pathology Vision (Input: LeafSample)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On-device MobileNet-v3 / ConvNeXt lightweight neural network trained on PlantVillage paddy pathology datasets.

Variables:
- Pathogen Severity Score : 0 (Clean) to 100 (Full blast lesion coverage)
- Model Confidence Level  : e.g., 96.4%
- Diagnosed Pathogen      : Magnaporthe oryzae (Rice Blast), Bipolaris oryzae (Brown Spot), Xanthomonas oryzae (Bacterial Blight), Healthy.

Leaf Risk Computation:
L_risk = PathogenSeverity * (Confidence / 100)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 Unified Multi-Modal Hazard Scoring Formula
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The overall field risk is calculated using weighted tri-modal linear fusion:

Total Risk (%) = (0.35 * S_risk) + (0.35 * I_risk) + (0.30 * L_risk)
Overall Health Score = max(5, min(100, 100 - Total Risk))
AI Confidence Score  = 88.0 + (HealthScore * 0.10) + (Random Normal Noise)

Classification Thresholds:
- Health Score >= 80%           -> Healthy / Low Risk
- Health Score 60% - 79%        -> Moderate Anomaly / Preventive Advisory
- Health Score 40% - 59%        -> Elevated Pest / Disease Risk (Active Spray Required)
- Health Score < 40%            -> Severe Water Stress / Critical Pathological Threat

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 Explainable AI (XAI) Factor Attribution (SHAP-Style Logic)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Located at: `src/components/Analytics/XaiAttributionMatrix.tsx`

To prevent AI from being a "black box" to agronomists and agricultural extension officers:
- Spectral Factor Weight : (S_risk / Total_Sum) * 100 -> e.g., 38% (Canopy vigor deficit & NDMI loss)
- IoT Telemetry Weight   : (I_risk / Total_Sum) * 100 -> e.g., 34% (Subsoil moisture deficit @ 18% VWC)
- Edge Visual Weight     : (L_risk / Total_Sum) * 100 -> e.g., 28% (Spindle-shaped fungal lesions)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Soil Nutrient Deficit & Commercial Bag Math
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard Agronomic Recommendation for Paddy/Rice: 120 kg N : 60 kg P2O5 : 40 kg K2O per Hectare.

Mathematical Logic:
Given:
- Field Area (Acres): `A` (1 Hectare = 2.471 Acres)
- Live Soil Sensor Telemetry:
    Nitrogen (N) current       : `telemetry.nitrogen` (mg/kg, optimal = 45 mg/kg)
    Phosphorus (P) current     : `telemetry.phosphorus` (mg/kg, optimal = 25 mg/kg)
    Potassium (K) current      : `telemetry.potassium` (mg/kg, optimal = 40 mg/kg)

Deficit Percentages:
- N_deficit = max(0.10, min(1.0, (45 - current_N) / 45))
- P_deficit = max(0.10, min(1.0, (25 - current_P) / 25))
- K_deficit = max(0.10, min(1.0, (40 - current_K) / 40))

Elemental Required (kg):
- Total_N_kg = A * 48.5 * N_deficit
- Total_P_kg = A * 24.3 * P_deficit
- Total_K_kg = A * 16.2 * K_deficit

Commercial Fertilizer Conversions:
1. Di-Ammonium Phosphate (DAP, 18-46-0):
   DAP provides both Phosphorus (46% P2O5) and Nitrogen (18% N).
   DAP_Total_kg = Total_P_kg / 0.46
   DAP_Bags (50 kg) = ceil(DAP_Total_kg / 50)
   N_Supplied_by_DAP = DAP_Total_kg * 0.18

2. Neem Coated Urea (46% N):
   Remaining_N = max(0, Total_N_kg - N_Supplied_by_DAP)
   Urea_Total_kg = Remaining_N / 0.46
   Urea_Bags (45 kg) = ceil(Urea_Total_kg / 45)

3. Muriate of Potash (MOP, 60% K2O):
   MOP_Total_kg = Total_K_kg / 0.60
   MOP_Bags (50 kg) = ceil(MOP_Total_kg / 50)

Subsidized Direct Input Cost (INR ₹):
Total Cost = (Urea_Bags * ₹266.50) + (DAP_Bags * ₹1,350.00) + (MOP_Bags * ₹1,700.00)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 Split-Dose Crop Staging Calendar Logic
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To prevent nitrogen leaching and ammonia volatilization:
1. Basal Transplanting (Day 0) : 50% DAP + 100% MOP + 25% Urea + 5 kg Zinc Sulphate (21%)
2. Active Tillering (Day 25)   : 50% Urea top-dress with thin water layer (2-3 cm)
3. Panicle Initiation (Day 50) : 25% Urea balance top-dress for grain filling

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 Pathogen-Specific Chemical & Bio-Control Dosage Logic
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Located at: `src/components/Agronomy/PestRxCard.tsx`

If Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae) detected:
- Chemical Rx   : Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g / Litre of water (or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 1.5 ml/L).
                  Spray Volume: 200 Litres / Acre via hollow cone nozzle.
- Bio-Control Rx: Pseudomonas fluorescens @ 10 g / Litre or Trichoderma viride @ 5 g / L.
- Safe Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 21 Days.

5.2 WMO Weather Interpretation & Dynamic Spray Window Advisory
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WMO Weather Codes:
- 0, 1        : Clear Sky / Mainly Clear -> High sunlight, good photosynthetically active radiation (PAR).
- 2, 3        : Partly Cloudy / Overcast.
- 51-65, 80-82: Drizzle / Heavy Rain Surge -> Washout hazard.
- 95-99       : Thunderstorm Warning -> Immediate halt.

Agronomic Spray Feasibility Formula:
- If Rain > 0.5 mm OR Wind Speed > 20 km/h:
    Advisory = "Unfavorable / छिड़काव न करें (तेज़ हवा / बारिश का खतरा)"
- If Wind Speed > 12 km/h OR Humidity > 85% OR Humidity < 35%:
    Advisory = "Caution / सावधानीपूर्वक स्प्रे करें (हवा / उच्च नमी)"
- Else:
    Advisory = "Optimal / छिड़काव के लिए सर्वोत्तम समय (Good Agronomy Window)"

6. 14-DAY TEMPORAL TRAJECTORY FORECASTING MODEL
====================================================================================================
Located at: `src/components/Analytics/TemporalForecastChart.tsx`

Mathematical modeling comparing two parallel futures across a 14-day window (Day -7 to Day +7):

1. Untreated Pathological Loss Curve (Red):
   NDVI(t) = NDVI_0 * exp(-k_decay * (t - t_infection))
   Risk(t) = min(98%, Risk_0 + (1 - exp(-0.18 * t)) * (100 - Risk_0))
   Yield Loss Projection: Up to -38.5% at harvest.

2. Early AI Intervention Recovery Curve (Emerald):
   Following Tricyclazole fungicide / balanced NPK application at t = 0:
   NDVI(t) = NDVI_current + (NDVI_healthy - NDVI_current) * (1 / (1 + exp(-0.6 * (t - 3))))
   Risk(t) = max(12%, Risk_current * exp(-0.42 * t))
   Yield Recovery: +18.2% harvest yield preserved.
